How it all started

Recovery made the problem impossible to ignore.

Why did support disappear every time Ana changed position?

During surgical recovery in June 2025, Ana struggled to find a pillow that supported both her neck and the way she actually slept.

Why was adjustment so much work?

The available options stayed fixed or required removing and rearranging fill. Neither felt natural for everyday use.

Could a pillow adapt instead?

At Boston University, Ana and Dannar turned that question into Somni. The team joined the Innovate@BU accelerator and began listening, prototyping, and learning.

One lived problem.50+ conversations.One idea worth testing.

Quick answers

What is Somni?

Clear answers about the product we are developing.

What makes Somni different from regular pillows?+

Most pillows have a fixed height and firmness, forcing you to adapt to them. Somni is designed to do the opposite, allowing you to adjust your pillow’s height and support to match your individual comfort needs.

How does Somni work?+

Somni uses adjustable air chambers to change the pillow’s height and support. Our first version, AXIS Core, is designed for manual adjustment, while our future AXIS Smart concept aims to make adjustments automatically as you change sleeping positions.

Who is Somni designed for?+

Somni is designed for people who struggle to find a comfortable pillow, frequently adjust their pillows throughout the night, or experience discomfort from inadequate neck support.

Can Somni help with neck pain?+

Somni is designed to provide customizable neck and head support, which may help improve sleeping comfort. However, Somni is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose or treat neck pain.

When will Somni be available?+

We’re currently developing and testing our prototypes. While we don’t have an official launch date yet, you can join our waitlist to receive updates and learn about upcoming testing opportunities.
